## Research

Eight numbered sources are linked in the article: the Ministry of Culture's 2016 National Inventory entry, UNESCO safeguarding file 01611, the Domna Samiou archive's 1989 recording of Alismono kai chairomai, ERT archive record 6947, ASCSA/Gennadius Library's 30 November 2022 event, the Domna Samiou archive's Paris 1983 / Ocora 1984 album entry, ERT's 2017 announcement of Ta avrio tis polyfonias, and the Domna Samiou archive's Paris 1983 recording of Kalotycha einai ta vouna.

Keep UNESCO's 2020 recognition of the Polyphonic Caravan's safeguarding programme distinct from the 2016 National Inventory entry for the musical practice. Describe roles with local variation; do not claim every ensemble uses every role or that all polyphonic singing has exactly the same structure. The vocabulary diagram is an original explanatory graphic made with semantic HTML and CSS, not a musical score or a claim about a particular ensemble.

Sokratis Tsiavos's testimony is reproduced in Greek in the National Inventory entry, historical section. Only the short final sentence is quoted verbatim; the context is paraphrased. The brief traditional lyric is taken from the archive's 1989 song record. The prose linking these to migration and memory is editorial reflection, not invented personal testimony.

The Paris session is dated 1983 and the album release 1984 following the recording archive itself. The National Inventory's historical account uses 1984 for the Paris visit; do not conflate release and recording dates. The ERT series dates are 1976–1977; no unverified exact episode date is supplied. The Ktismata segment's 19:07 timestamp comes from the archive's analysis, not direct audiovisual review.
