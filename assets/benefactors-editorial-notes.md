# Ηπειρώτες ευεργέτες — editorial record

Prepared 27 October 2026 for evergetes.html.

## Owner preferences

- Keep the existing photo header verbatim, including assets/card-benefactors-ai.webp, and preserve the existing portal card in ipeiros.html. The owner explicitly asked to keep both images. The header is decorative; it is not presented as historical evidence.
- Use flowing Greek narrative. Lead with information, place numbered references after the relevant passage, and name sources in the bibliography. Do not invent testimony, recollections, motives, or quotations.
- Add genuine historical portraits within the article, with provenance and rights.

## Research and factual choices

Fourteen linked references accompany the article, including the benefactors' own educational institutions, university histories, museum archival catalogues, National Documentation Centre, the Panathenaic Stadium and Zappeion. Direct quotations are restricted to a short excerpt from Georgios Rizaris, as reproduced by the Rizarios heritage site, and eight words from Stournaris's will as reproduced by NTUA. Historical facts are paraphrased; reflective passages are editorial prose, not reported testimony.

Avoid conflicting birth dates for Arsakis and the Zosimas brothers. Avoid a precise Kaplaneios founding year because accounts differ. Distinguish Rizarios Foundation (1841) from the school's opening (1844), and NTUA's foundation (1837) from the later donors' buildings. Attribute Eleni Tositsa's museum contribution to the land, not the entire construction. Distinguish the Zappas Olympia of 1859 from the modern Olympics of 1896. No portrait is substituted for Eleni Tositsa, Angeliki Papazoglou, Nikolaos Stournaris or Georgios Hatzikostas. The often-found portrait of revolutionary Nikolaos Stournaris represents a different person and must not be used here.
