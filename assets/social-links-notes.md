# Association social shortcuts

Added 27 October 2026 at the owner's request. The source is the association's own website, https://ipirotikos.gr/ , also repeated at https://ipirotikos.gr/epikoinonia/ .

Confirmed destination URLs extracted from its social links:

- Facebook group: https://www.facebook.com/groups/274979100353/
- YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCBxJJNTRyrIDn_vw1ux9XTQ

Its Twitter and Instagram links currently lead only to https://twitter.com/ and https://www.instagram.com/ . These generic platform homepages are not association profiles and are deliberately not added as shortcuts. Add them when an actual association profile URL is verified. Automated viewing of the confirmed Facebook group and YouTube channel was limited by platform login/throttling; affiliation is established by the links published on the official association website, not an invented handle or a similarly named account.

Both confirmed shortcuts are static anchors in the top bar and footer of all 23 full HTML pages. draseis.html is a legacy redirect to arxeio.html and has no shared navigation to update. Icons use inline SVG and currentColor, with descriptive accessible link names and visible platform labels in the footer. No icon CDN, font download, social tracking script or JavaScript injection is needed. External shortcuts open in a new tab with noopener noreferrer. Styling lives in assets/theme.css; all pages use the updated social-links-15 cache version.

Validation: all 23 full pages contain exactly two social navigation blocks, each with the two correct links, SVGs and accessibility attributes. Article headers and all main content were checked against the original strings and remain unchanged. Existing header/card images and pending wedding assets are untouched. CSS braces and shared theme links were checked. Browser rendering was not run: Chromium launch was previously blocked by the session sandbox.
