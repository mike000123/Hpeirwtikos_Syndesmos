# Discover Epirus: clear article headers

27 October 2026. The owner requested no diffuse red or burgundy tint at all on the six article headers linked from «Μια πατρίδα, χίλιες ιστορίες» (#discover in ipeiros.html).

The six pages are topoi.html, mousiki.html, istoria.html, ethima.html, texnes.html and geuseis.html. Each header now has the explicit article-hero-clear class and loads assets/discover-headers.css. This removes both pseudo-element overlays at every viewport width and keeps images fully opaque without filters or blend modes. White lettering uses only neutral black text shadows; no coloured veil, gradient or title panel covers the photograph.

The portal cards are unchanged. Four header image URLs already matched their cards and are retained. istoria.html and ethima.html still used older Unsplash imagery, so they now reuse their existing portal-card images assets/history-people-square.webp and assets/customs-village-festival.webp, with the same 43% and 48% focal positions. No new imagery was created or selected.

Only this Discover collection is affected. Other folklore/archive header treatments remain unchanged, as do social links, article prose and shared styles. Future changes should preserve each article's existing card image and keep these six headers free of tint.
