# Οι δρόμοι της ξενιτιάς — editorial record

Prepared 27 October 2026 for mike000123/Hpeirwtikos_Syndesmos.

The feature contains an original Greek narrative and clearly attributed summaries of documented material. The bibliography in xenitia.html is the canonical reader-facing source list. Historical claims have numbered links; reflective passages do not purport to be personal testimony. No invented quotations, composite characters, attributed interior thoughts, synthetic historical portraits, regional migration statistics, or claims that every family shared the same experience are used.

## Research and claim boundaries

- Official association history, https://ipirotikos.gr/ : migration destinations, 3 June 1886 foundation, 1899 renaming and April 1932 club opening.
- Eleftheria I. Nikolaidou, Dodoni 15 (1986), pp. 103–128, https://olympias.lib.uoi.gr/jspui/handle/123456789/6145 : places of origin, Russian Empire cities and forms of philanthropy from the institutional abstract/metadata. The full article was not used to establish detailed claims. Do not reproduce the apparent century error in the English abstract or convert historical monetary totals.
- NTUA/MIRC, https://www.mirc.ntua.gr/foundation/db/epirus_db/ARXITEKTONIKH/Mastores.htm : regions of craftsmen, work teams, seasonal departure/return and recorded water custom. Local custom is not generalized to all villages.
- UNESCO file 01611, https://ich.unesco.org/en/BSP/polyphonic-caravan-researching-safeguarding-and-promoting-the-epirus-polyphonic-song-01611 : migration and loss of village performers, Chaonia and 2020 Register of Good Safeguarding Practices selection. The recognition concerns the safeguarding project, not an assertion that all Epirus music was inscribed in the Representative List.
- Ioannis Zelepos, bpb, 23 January 2017; Monika Mattes, bpb, 8 April 2019; German federal historical overview; WDR Kalimera Deutschland: German recruitment, housing and women's labor history. This is wider Greek/international context, not Epirus-specific numerical evidence. The precise 30 March 1960 agreement date is supported by WDR.
- ERT archive item 8320, https://archive.ert.gr/8320/ : archival description at 00:34:58 onwards records Michalis Ganas recalling Albania/Hungary during 1948–1954 and reading Mitria Patrida. No recording or literary passage is copied. War displacement is distinguished from labor migration.
- University of Sydney OMAA catalogue items 2855, 2861 and 2862: summaries of State Library of NSW Greek-Australian Archive interviews by Nick Doumanis. Facts used: Evangelia Athanassiou (Souli, 1962), Ioannis Dimou (Pigi, 1932; arrival 1962, Brittany), John Dimitriou (Konitsa; arrival 1964, Patris). These are paraphrases of catalogue summaries, not transcripts or claims to have listened to the recordings. Search-indexed catalogue descriptions were accessible; direct OMAA page opens timed out during preparation.
- Our People Their Stories, https://opts.org.au/videos/the-man-from-the-craft-villages-of-konitsa-who-carved-his-own-future-in-the-fifth-continent/ : separate Ioannis Dimou video record, linked without copying its audiovisual content.
- Themis Chatzigeorgiou, I apodimia ton Ipeiroton (1958), https://olympias.lib.uoi.gr/jspui/handle/123456789/25104 : digitized text, especially printed pp. 86–90, for folklore of absence and return. Literary anecdotes in the book are not passed off as verified biographies. Author and year also checked against NTUA bibliographic catalogue and Greek government library bibliography.
- G. Ch. Chasiotis (1866), Apohorismos, no. 2 in Chasiotis section of https://users.sch.gr/ipap/Ellinikos%20Politismos/Yliko/mou/xenitia-tragoudia.htm : a nine-word traditional lyric excerpt. No modern recording is embedded.
